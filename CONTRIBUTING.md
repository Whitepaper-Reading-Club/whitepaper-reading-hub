# Contribute to the reading hub

## Suggest a paper

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Whitepaper-Reading-Club/whitepaper-reading-hub/issues/new) with the paper title, author, original source link, and a short explanation of why the club should read it.

## Post notes or a transcript

1. Use the templates in [`templates/`](templates/) to prepare your material.
2. Add a paper page at `papers/paper-slug.md` if one does not exist.
3. Add session files under `sessions/YYYY-MM-DD-paper-slug/`.
4. Link the paper in [`papers/README.md`](papers/README.md), link the session in [`sessions/README.md`](sessions/README.md), and connect the paper page to its sessions.
5. Submit a pull request describing what you added or corrected. A maintainer can review and merge it.

You can contribute in the browser: open the destination folder, choose **Add file → Create new file** or **Upload files**, and propose your changes. If you do not have write access, GitHub will guide you through creating a fork and pull request. For a small correction, open a file and use the edit button.

## Content guidelines

- Cite the original paper and include page or section references where useful.
- Separate paper claims from discussion, interpretation, and open questions.
- Mark transcripts as verbatim, edited, or machine-generated, and state whether they were reviewed.
- Use timestamps when available and mark uncertain words instead of guessing.
- Confirm speaker permission and remove private information before publishing.
- Credit contributors with their permission. Include only material you can share publicly.

To report a correction without editing files, open an issue with a link to the relevant passage and the proposed change.
