## Overview

Reading of New Constellation version 1.0, Multiple Concurrent Proposers for Alpenglow, with the authors present. In the transcript they are labelled Roger and Quentin. Those labels are best-effort and are explained in the transcript.

The discussion covers bundles and priority-fee ordering, gossip versus on-chain registration, denial of service, duplicate transactions and inclusion fees, skipping and slashing, how hubs are formed, malicious proposers and hub stake, what multiple concurrent proposers are for, the proposer schedule and the choice of *k*, the last-revealer attack, transaction privacy, and performance.
